## Observations

1. **Comprehensive analytics**: Multiple analytical views
2. **Clinical focus**: Medication, QOF, Safeguarding, Clinical Guardian
3. **NHS integration**: SCR, NCRs for national health records
4. **Comms Analytics**: External link to NHS Comms Hub
5. **Role-based access**: "not authorized" message for SCR report
6. **Capacity planning**: Capacity & Demand for resource planning
