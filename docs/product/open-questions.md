## Format

Mỗi question:
- Stable ID: `OQ-XXX`
- Status: Open/Answered/Obsolete
- Question text
- Impact: Critical/High/Medium/Low
- Evidence và related observations
- Answer source (khi answered)
- Date created, date answered
